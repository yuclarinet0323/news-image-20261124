"The World Today" is a one-image newspaper front page summing up the world news of Tuesday 24 November 2026, drawn entirely by a Python program (standard library only, hand-rolled PNG encoder and bitmap font, fully deterministic). The lead story is the US jury verdict finding Huawei guilty of trade-secret theft and bank fraud while acquitting it of racketeering. Inside panels cover Iran saying tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz and refusing talks until the US blockade is lifted, Ukrainian drones hitting Russian ships in the Gulf of Finland and off Libya, Malaysia returning 6,500 Rohingya to Myanmar days after the ICJ genocide ruling, Pakistan's Zeeshan Ali becoming the first foreign astronaut to fly to China's Tiangong station on Shenzhou-24, and the Banten candy factory fire toll rising to 26 dead, plus a ticker with Senegal regaining the AFCON title on appeal, Democrats reaching 259 House seats, and UK sanctions on the Houthis.
